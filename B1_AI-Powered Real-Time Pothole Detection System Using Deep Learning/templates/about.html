About Potholes

Potholes are depressions or holes formed on road surfaces due to water infiltration, heavy traffic, and pavement deterioration. They can cause vehicle damage, increase maintenance costs, and lead to serious accidents. Detecting potholes in real time helps authorities repair roads quickly and improve public safety.

About Cracks

Road cracks are fractures that develop because of aging, temperature changes, and excessive traffic loads. If left untreated, these cracks gradually expand and become potholes. Early crack detection reduces road maintenance costs and extends the lifespan of transportation infrastructure.
